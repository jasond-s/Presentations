What is Shape Up?

  • Development methodology created by Basecamp
  • Alternative to traditional Agile practices
  • Focus on shipping meaningful work in cycles

Core Principles

Shaping

Define the solution space, not the solution

Betting

Commit to projects by your appetite in time

Core Principles

Building

Complete autonomy for implementation teams

Downtime

Space between projects to think

Shaping Work

  • Fat marker: High-level designs
  • Rabbit holes: Clear boundaries and constraints
  • Appetite: How much of our time is worth it
  • Leave room for creativity in implementation

Cycles

  • Long enough for meaningful work
  • Short enough to see the end
  • No sprints or story points
  • Cool-down period between cycles

No Backlogs

  • Start fresh each cycle
  • Good ideas resurface naturally
  • Reduces psychological burden
  • Forces prioritization

Team Methodology

  • Work it out yourself
